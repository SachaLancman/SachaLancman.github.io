About the atelier

Small studio, big curiosity

Atelier is French for workshop: a place where things are made by hand, with care. That's the spirit here.

Atelier Sacha builds small games that are quick to pick up, kind to your time and fun to come back to. Every game, drawing and word list is made in-house.
